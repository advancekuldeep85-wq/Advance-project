About Mahatma Jyotiba Phule Rohilkhand University CSIT Department

Who We Are

The Computer Science & IT (CSIT) Department at Mahatma Jyotiba Phule Rohilkhand University, Bareilly is committed to providing world-class education in computer science and information technology.

Our mission is to develop skilled professionals who can contribute to the growth of the IT industry and society.

Our Vision

To be a leading department in computer science education, fostering innovation, research, and excellence.

We focus on practical learning, industry collaboration, and holistic development of our students.
